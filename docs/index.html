What it is

Play something.

Rough Cuts is a monthly open jam at The Talleyrand in Levenshulme. Bring an instrument, put your name down, and get thrown together with people you haven't met to play something nobody has rehearsed. There's a house drum kit and vocal mics, so you only need to bring yourself and whatever you play. Entry is free, the bar is open, and every level is welcome, from first-timers to lifers. Some of it will be rough. That's the point.
